About us

QuSpectra is developing portable quantum sensing instruments based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond. We are compressing laboratory-scale quantum sensing and spectroscopy systems into compact, board-level instruments that deliver high-performance measurements in a portable form factor. Our technology combines quantum sensors, photonics, microwave electronics, embedded systems, and microfluidics to enable next-generation analytical tools.
